# Community Appendix: Doginal Dogs

Doginal Dogs are the inaugural Community Pass integration for CLAW.

They represent a high-signal, crypto-native social layer that aligns with
CLAW’s values: transparency, receipts, and voluntary participation.

---

## Eligibility Rules

A wallet qualifies as a Starter Dog if:

- It holds ≥1 Doginal Dog NFT
- The NFT is verified on the Doginal Dogs marketplace
- (Optional) The wallet owner links an X profile via signed message

No custody is required.  
No exclusivity is enforced.

---

## Starter Dog Perks

Starter Dogs receive the following **non-truth-affecting benefits**:

- Increased free daily signature quota
- “Starter Dog” badge on receipts
- Doginal Dogs–themed share cards
- Priority inclusion within batch epochs

Priority affects **ordering**, not anchoring guarantees.

---

## X Profile Linking (Optional)

Starter Dogs may link an X profile by:

- signing a message containing their X handle, or
- completing an OAuth flow (if enabled)

Linked profiles appear on public receipts **only if the user opts in**.

---

## Social Intent

Doginal Dogs integration is designed to:

- turn receipts into social artifacts
- reward early participation
- enable viral sharing without sacrificing truth

Doginal Dogs do not receive exclusive access to:
- signing
- verification
- anchoring
- disputes

Truth remains neutral.

---

## Future Extensions

The Doginal Dogs configuration serves as a template
for onboarding additional NFT communities across chains.

No core protocol changes are required.
