# INTERPRETATION — CLAW v1.x

## Purpose

This document governs how the CLAW v1.x protocol and its documentation are to be interpreted.

It exists to prevent semantic drift, back-projection of intent, or reinterpretation of implemented behavior.

---

## Interpretive Baseline

CLAW v1.x behavior and meaning are **frozen** as of release **v1.2.0**.

All interpretations of CLAW v1.x MUST be derived from:
- implemented code behavior,
- canonical documentation in `/docs`,
- and cryptographic artifacts produced under v1.x rules.

Later documents, commentary, or features MUST NOT reinterpret or expand the meaning of v1.x.

---

## Normative Control Documents

In the event of ambiguity, the following documents are **normative and controlling** for CLAW v1.x:

- `docs/COURT_SUMMARY.md`
- `docs/SEMANTIC_LOCK.md`
- `docs/VERIFICATION_MODEL.md`
- `docs/ANCHORING_MODEL.md`
- `docs/CLAW-PROTOCOL-LAYERS.md`

Examples, diagrams, blog posts, UI text, whitepapers, or AI-generated explanations are **non-normative**.

---

## Prohibited Inferences

Nothing in CLAW v1.x shall be interpreted as implying:

- adjudicative authority  
- legal judgment or ruling  
- enforcement power  
- truth determination  
- governance authority  
- substitution for courts, judges, or due process  

CLAW v1.x produces **cryptographic proofs and verification results only**.

Any legal or practical effect arises solely from **external law or voluntary agreement**, not from CLAW itself.

---

## Forward Compatibility

Future versions (v2+) may introduce new capabilities, semantics, or protocol layers.

Such changes:
- require new version identifiers,
- MUST NOT retroactively alter v1.x meaning,
- and MUST be interpreted independently.

CLAW v1.x artifacts remain governed by v1.x rules indefinitely.

---

## Rule of Construction

If a term, description, or usage is ambiguous:

> **Interpretation shall resolve against authority, adjudication, or enforcement claims.**

---

## Status

This document is **normative** for CLAW v1.x as of release **v1.2.0**.
