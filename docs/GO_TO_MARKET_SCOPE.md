# CLAW v1 Go‑To‑Market Scope

## What CLAW v1 is ready for
- Evidence capture and sharing for attestations and timelines
- Repo‑less verification of integrity and sequence
- Court‑safe, audit‑grade evidence packaging

## What is explicitly deferred
- Agreement adjudication or enforcement
- Any legal advice or jurisdictional claims
- Any outcome or decision issuance

## How lawyers and counterparties should use it
- Treat CLAW v1 outputs as evidence records only
- Verify using `verify.py` locally
- Cite timeline and receipt identifiers when referencing records
