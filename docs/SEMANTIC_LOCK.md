# SEMANTIC_LOCK — CLAW v1.x Meaning Preservation

## Status
**Normative for CLAW v1.x**  
This document is binding on interpretation of CLAW v1.x behavior and claims.
This document is normative for CLAW v1.x as of release v1.2.0.

---

## Purpose

The purpose of this Semantic Lock is to **freeze the meaning** of CLAW v1.x and prevent:

- retroactive reinterpretation
- implied authority creep
- feature inference from future documents
- argumentative expansion by analogy or implication

This document ensures that **what CLAW v1.x means today is what it will always mean**, regardless of future development.

---

## Core Rule (Binding)

> **CLAW v1.x SHALL be interpreted only according to what is explicitly implemented, documented, and verified in v1.x artifacts.**

No capability, authority, or intent may be inferred from:
- future roadmap documents
- aspirational architecture descriptions
- naming conventions
- marketing language
- analogies to courts, judges, arbitration, or enforcement systems

Silence does not imply functionality.

---

## Locked Scope of CLAW v1.x

CLAW v1.x is limited to the following **exclusive scope**:

- cryptographic proof of existence
- cryptographic proof of sequence
- cryptographic proof of data integrity
- deterministic, third-party verification

Nothing outside this scope exists in CLAW v1.x.

---

## Explicit Exclusions (Semantic Firewall)

CLAW v1.x does **not**:

- determine truth or falsity
- assess legality or compliance
- adjudicate disputes
- issue rulings, judgments, or decisions
- authenticate identity by itself
- compel or enforce action
- hold, transfer, or control assets
- substitute for courts, arbitrators, or lawyers
- create obligations absent external agreement or law

These exclusions are **intentional and permanent** for v1.x.

---

## Terminology Constraints

The following terms, when used in CLAW v1.x documentation or code, **MUST be interpreted narrowly**:

- **“verify”** → cryptographic integrity checking only  
- **“receipt”** → proof object, not acknowledgment or consent  
- **“anchor”** → time-bounding via public ledger, not finality or endorsement  
- **“proof”** → mathematical proof of data properties, not legal proof  
- **“timeline”** → ordered data structure, not factual narrative  

No term implies legal authority, judgment, or enforceability.

---

## Version Boundary Rule

Features or concepts described in any document labeled:

- “v2”
- “future”
- “aspirational”
- “non-normative”
- “conceptual”

**DO NOT apply to CLAW v1.x**, even if:
- technically feasible
- partially implemented elsewhere
- commonly associated with similar systems

Each major capability expansion requires:
- a new protocol identifier
- explicit documentation
- non-retroactive semantics

---

## Conflict Resolution Rule

If any ambiguity or conflict arises:

1. Prefer the **narrowest interpretation**
2. Defer to:
   - `COURT_SUMMARY.md`
   - `VERIFICATION_MODEL.md`
   - `ANCHORING_MODEL.md`
3. Reject implied authority or intent

CLAW favors under-claiming over over-claiming.

---

## Legal Interpretation Statement

CLAW v1.x is a **technical recordkeeping and verification system**.

Any legal effect arises solely from:
- external law
- independent agreements
- human judgment

CLAW itself is **non-authoritative**.

---

## Permanence

This Semantic Lock applies indefinitely to CLAW v1.x.

Future versions:
- may extend functionality
- may introduce new semantics
- may add authority *only if explicitly defined*

They **cannot** alter or reinterpret CLAW v1.x.
