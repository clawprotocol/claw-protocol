# CLAW v1 Product Boundary

## What v1 is
- Evidence-first workflow for creating, freezing, exporting, and verifying artifacts.
- Deterministic, file-based verification that is independent of servers.
- Exportable and appeal-compatible records intended for review by counterparties and auditors.

## What v1 is not
- Not legal advice, legal judgment, or jurisdictional authority.
- Not enforcement or outcome determination.
- Not an on-chain anchoring system (no blockchain calls in v1).

## Non-binding outputs
- All outputs are informational evidence artifacts by default.
- Verification only confirms integrity and linkage of files.

## Evidence vs notes
- **Evidence** is exportable, verifiable, and intended for review.
- **Notes** are private structuring aids and are not part of the evidentiary record by default.
- Notes are not legal advice and should not be treated as legal conclusions.

## Scope of responsibility
- CLAW v1 guarantees cryptographic integrity when verified.
- CLAW v1 does not guarantee truthfulness, intent, or legal enforceability.
