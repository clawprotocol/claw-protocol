# CLAW Pricing & Freemium Model

## Why Pricing Exists

CLAW does not charge for truth.

It charges for:
- speed
- certainty
- enforceability
- escalation readiness

Signing is free because it is the gateway.
Depth is paid because it creates gravity.

---

## The Ladder (Mental Model)

L0 — Free / Viral  
L1 — Pro  
L2 — Legal / Institutional  
L3 — Protocol / Infrastructure  

Each level unlocks **depth**, not friction.

---

## L0 — Free / Viral (Default Entry)

This tier is optimized for:
- massive scale
- minimal treasury burn
- zero explanation required

### What users get (Free)

- E-sign documents or text
- Instant verifiable receipt
- Public verification link
- Off-chain verification
- Inclusion in batch anchoring epochs

### What CLAW pays (Treasury-Safe)

- No per-user gas
- No per-signature on-chain writes
- One shared anchor per epoch (Merkle root)

Anchoring cadence at launch may be:
- every 3–7 days, or
- once a batch threshold is reached

This keeps freemium cost **well under 0.1% of treasury** even at very small balances.

---

## Abuse Protection (Critical)

To protect the treasury:

- Per-wallet daily limits
- Per-IP rate limits
- Progressive friction for abuse
  - captcha
  - proof-of-work
  - temporary throttling

No fees unless behavior forces them.

---

## L1 — Pro

For users who want speed and reliability.

### Unlocks

- Higher daily signing limits
- Faster anchoring cadence (e.g. daily)
- Multi-party signing flows
- Exportable verification bundles
- Priority epoch inclusion

This tier funds faster anchors for everyone.

---

## L2 — Legal / Institutional

For lawyers, organizations, and regulated actors.

### Unlocks

- Role-gated signing (lawyers, officers)
- Jurisdictional metadata enforcement
- Policy registration
- Dispute packet generation
- Team and organization controls

This tier is where CLAW becomes operational infrastructure.

---

## L3 — Protocol / Infrastructure

For systems that need inevitability.

### Unlocks

- Challenge windows
- Counter-attestation flows
- Automated narrative packets
- Precedent and evidence graphs
- DAO, court, and regulator integrations

---

## Treasury Safety Rule (Non-Negotiable)

Freemium operation must always:

- batch signatures
- anchor shared roots
- throttle abuse
- scale cadence only with revenue

CLAW never risks solvency for growth.

---

## Summary

Signing is free.

Proof is cheap.

Certainty is valuable.

Enforcement is inevitable.
