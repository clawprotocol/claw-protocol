CLAW Draft Clause — Mutual Non-Disparagement & Corrections (v0)

1. Non-Disparagement
Each Party agrees not to knowingly publish false statements of fact about the other Party.

2. Corrections
If a Party becomes aware it has published a false statement of fact, it will publish a correction within seventy-two (72) hours.

3. Carve-Outs
This clause does not restrict:
(a) truthful statements,
(b) legal filings,
(c) communications with legal counsel.

4. Survival
This clause survives termination for twenty-four (24) months.

Status: Draft. Not legal advice. Jurisdiction-agnostic.
